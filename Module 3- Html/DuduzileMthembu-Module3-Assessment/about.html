About Little Stars Daycare Centre

Founded in 2018, Little Stars Daycare Centre was created with one mission:
to provide a nurturing home-away-from-home for children in Soweto.

We believe every child is a'little star'with unique talents. Our team of trained ECD practitioners focus on play-based learning, emotional development, and school readiness.

Our Mission

To inspire curiosity, build confidence, and lay a strong foundation for lifelong learning in a safe and loving space.

Our Values

  • Care: Every child is treated with love and respect
  • Growth: We encourage learning through play
  • Community: Partnering with parents for the best results
